## R-001 — Arabic normalization policy (ة → ه) — OPEN
Source: CORE-14 approval review (2026-09-09).
The Foundation's Arabic pack folds taa-marbuta (ة) into haa (ه) and strips harakat for comparison. This is a configurable policy decision, NOT a universal linguistic truth. It MUST become configurable policy (per activity/grade) before wide educational use.

## R-002 — MISSPELLING thresholds — OPEN
Source: CORE-14 approval review (2026-09-09).
Current misspelling thresholds are placeholders. They MUST NOT be treated as a finalized educational standard until validated against real student data.

## R-003 — STT as candidate-only, never source of truth — OPEN
Source: CORE-14 approval review (2026-09-09).
When audio is enabled, STT output is a candidate response only. The chain must remain:
Audio → STT → Candidate Response → Comparison → Measurement → Evidence
No direct provider calls; STT must never override comparison/measurement.
